The fastest way to miss quota is spending time on deals you cannot close. Answer all 6 questions before investing more than 2 hours.

Step 2 — Open Every Deal at the Top
Start at VP Asset Management or COO level. Do not let yourself get stuck at manager level.
Managers cannot approve multi-year SaaS contracts — you will need to re-sell upward anyway
Executive relationships compress sales cycles — a VP can unlock 6 months of procurement in one conversation
Starting at the top signals partnership, not vendor status
Door opener: "We just completed a 5-year agreement with PG&E — I would like to share what drove that decision with you directly." That sentence opens executive doors.
Step 3 — Run a Structured Discovery
Discovery is not fact-finding. It is pain amplification. Goal: get the prospect to articulate their pain in their own words.
PhaseGoalKey Question
Current stateUnderstand today's process"Walk me through your current vegetation management and inspection process end to end."
Pain identificationGet them to name the problem"Where does that process break down? What keeps you up at night about it?"
Impact quantificationPut a number on the pain"What does that cost you in dollars, hours, or regulatory exposure?"
Future stateDefine what good looks like"If you solved this perfectly, what would that mean for your operations?"
Political mapIdentify who else is involved"Who else is affected by this? Who would be part of the decision?"
Compelling eventFind the forcing function"Is there a deadline or regulatory cycle driving when this needs to be solved?"
Step 4 — Build and Enable Your Champion
Your champion is your internal seller. They present CORE when you are not in the room. A deal without a champion is a deal at risk.
Test influence: ask them to arrange a meeting with the EB. If they can't, they're not a real champion.
Arm them: one-pager with the ROI model in their specific numbers — something they can share internally
Coach objections: walk through every internal objection and give them the answer
Stay in contact: weekly check-ins to track internal momentum and surface blockers early
Never replace them: adding new stakeholders does not eliminate the need for your original champion

Step 6 — Close with a Compelling Event
Every deal needs a real reason to close by a specific date. Create urgency that is genuine — never manufactured.
Regulatory: "Your NERC FAC-003 cycle ends in Q3 — implementation takes 8 weeks. That means a decision by [date]."
Budget cycle: "Your budget year closes in October. To get this in this year's plan, procurement needs the contract by [date]."
Wildfire season: "Vegetation programs ramp in spring. To have CORE operational for peak season, we start implementation by [date]."
Executive sponsor: "Your COO said this is an H2 priority. To hit that timeline, here is what needs to happen by when."
The close question: "Based on everything we have discussed, do you see any reason this would not move forward on [date]?" This surfaces real objections before they become surprises.

Your forecast is your credibility. Every miss erodes trust. Every hit builds it. Accurate forecasting separates elite sellers from average ones.

Forecast Hygiene Rules — Non-Negotiable
Never put a deal in Commit without confirmed EB access — no exceptions
Never leave a deal in pipeline 60+ days without moving it forward or disqualifying it
Update CRM within 24 hours of any meaningful deal conversation
If you miss a committed deal, document why — build pattern recognition over time
Sandbag = lost trust. Stretch = lost trust. Accurate = earned trust.
Present upside and risk transparently — leadership respects honesty over optimism

Step 9 — Winning Competitive Deals
When Competition Enters — Six Steps
Identify immediately: "Are you evaluating other approaches or vendors?" Ask directly, early.
Understand their criteria: what the competitor is pitching tells you what matters most to the buyer
Reinforce your strengths: AI LiDAR intelligence, T&D-specific digital twin, Grid View, PG&E 5-year reference
Attack status quo first: most deals are lost to "do nothing," not to a named competitor
Coach your champion: get them to articulate your differentiation in their own words
Deploy the reference strategically: a PG&E call at the right moment closes competitive deals faster than any demo
Competitive Talk Tracks
If they say...Your response
"We use DroneDeploy"DroneDeploy captures data well. CORE turns that data into intelligence — automated LiDAR classification, vegetation proximity detection, and a company-wide digital twin connecting every asset to its full history. Most of our customers used basic drone capture before CORE. Want to see the difference?
"Happy with helicopter program"Most utilities said the same — until an audit or a vegetation outage. The question is whether you want to be proactive or reactive. What would it take for your current process to fail?
"Price is too high"Let us build the business case together. What is your current annual helicopter inspection spend? Most utilities find CORE pays for itself in year one just on inspection cost reduction — before NERC penalty avoidance.
"We have an internal solution"Internal tools are often great for initial capture. Does yours do automated LiDAR classification, GIS-matched digital twin, and vegetation proximity detection at scale? That is where utilities typically hit the wall.
